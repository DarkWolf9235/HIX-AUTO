Timing & Engine Repairs

Timing jobs are where a lot of shops charge the most and make you wait the longest. Hix Auto Worx takes on major engine work at your location, with the same care you'd expect from a dealer, without the tow bill or the week without your vehicle.

What we repair

  • Timing chains, guides & tensioners
  • Cam phasers & VVT solenoids
  • Water pumps & front covers
  • Valve cover & oil leak repair
  • Belts, pulleys & tensioners
  • Engine diagnostics & performance

Signs you need us

  • Rattle or chatter on cold start
  • Check engine light for cam or crank timing
  • Rough idle or misfires
  • Loss of power or poor fuel economy
  • Squeal or chirp from the belt
